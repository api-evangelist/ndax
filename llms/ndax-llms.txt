# Ndax - Canadian Cryptocurrency Exchange

> Ndax is a Canadian-regulated cryptocurrency exchange (FINTRAC-registered MSB and CIRO-regulated investment dealer) offering CAD on/off-ramps, spot trading, OTC services, staking, and a self-custody-friendly platform for individuals and businesses.

Headquartered in Calgary, Alberta. Serves all Canadian provinces and territories.
Supports 65+ cryptocurrencies with CAD trading pairs.
Regulated by FINTRAC (As a Money Services Business), registered as a Restricted Dealer with the CSA, and a member of the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO).
First Canadian cryptocurrency exchange certified SOC 2 Type II.
